GRID

GRID is IMERA's first game project: a multiplayer strategy-survival experience that combines skill-based resource gathering, territory control, army development, and competitive warfare.

Players develop their position, compete for resources, expand their territory, and build military strength. The ultimate goal is to eliminate opposing players by destroying their bases.
